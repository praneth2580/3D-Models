Minimal bezel

A 45 mm round front with a 4.25 mm ring around the polycarbonate lens. The lens sits 0.4 mm below a protective rim, and there are no front screws.

Quick-release bayonet

Push in, twist 30° clockwise, click. Three lugs run in L-shaped tracks, and a spring-ball detent preloads them so it doesn't rattle. One wider lug makes it fit only upright.

Sealed and serviceable

A flat gasket compressed 30 % seals the rear cover, which is held by three hidden M2 screws. A TPU plug closes the USB-C port.

Battery inside

A small LiPo sits in its own bay with 1 mm of room to swell, separated from the board by the retainer plate.

Board held without holes

The round board has no mounting holes, so a foam-backed retainer presses it onto a ledge behind the lens, with relief over the RST and BOOT buttons.

Handlebar clamp

A split clamp with a rubber shim and M5 bolts, and a receiver cup tilted toward the rider, with a drain at its lowest point.
