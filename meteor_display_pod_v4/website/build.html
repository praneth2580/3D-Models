Build it

  1. Measure your board, battery and handlebar, and update scad/parameters.scad.
  2. Print the fit test first: receiver and bayonet_test_male (about 1.5 h). Push in, twist, twist back and lift out, then tune entry_clear and track_clear if needed.
  3. Print the parts in PETG for the prototype, ASA for the final parts, and TPU for the plug and gasket. No PLA: it softens in the sun.
  4. Cut the 1 mm polycarbonate lens and the foam pieces from the DXF templates.
  5. Assemble: heat-set inserts, then bond the lens, fit the board, retainer and battery, close the cover, and fit the plunger, receiver and clamp.
  6. Test before riding: pull, vibration, heat (the board is rated to 70 °C) and water.

Hardware you'll need

  • ESP32-2424S012 1.28" round display board
  • LiPo cell with protection board (default 402030), JST 1.25 mm plug
  • 1 mm clear polycarbonate (lens)
  • M4 stainless spring-ball plunger
  • 3 × M2 × 8 screws + 3 × M2 heat-set inserts
  • 3 × M3 × 6–8 thread-forming screws
  • 2 × M5 × 25 screws + nyloc nuts
  • Thin foam, a rubber shim, clear RTV silicone
