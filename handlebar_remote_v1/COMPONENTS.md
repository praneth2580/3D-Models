# Components: what the remote is built (and modelled) around

**The printed parts are modelled around the items marked "Model fits this".** These are common hobby modules, made by many sellers with small differences, and none has a published drawing: measure yours and set the sizes in `handlebar_remote.scad` (the MEASURE lines) before printing.

**Prices:** estimates (not checked on a listing), marked (est.). Where to buy each item, with search links at the Indian stores: the project's page on the website ("What to buy").

| Part | Exact item | Model fits this? | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Joystick and buttons** | **"5-way navigation button module"** (also sold as "5D rocker joystick independent keyboard module"): a board about 41 × 26 mm with a 5-way joystick switch and 2 small push buttons (SET, RST), pins COM, UP, DWN, LFT, RHT, MID, SET, RST | **Yes** (MEASURE) | ₹60–150 (est.) |
| **Microcontroller** | **ESP32-C3 Super Mini** (22.52 × 18 mm, USB-C): Bluetooth LE and a CAN controller | **Yes** | ₹250–450 (est.) |
| **CAN transceiver** | **SN65HVD230 module** (the dash's 3-pack has a spare). Remove its 120 Ω resistor | **Yes** (MEASURE) | ₹150–300 (est.), or the dash's spare |
| **Cable gland** | M8 × 1.5 nylon cable gland, for a 3–6 mm cable | **Yes** | ₹20–60 (est.) |
| **Cable** | 4-core cable (shielded is better), about 1–1.5 m | No | ₹50–150 (est.) |
| **Plug, with the dash** | GX12 **6-pin** aviation plug (matches the dash's expansion slots) | No | ₹100–200 (est.) |
| **Plug, without the dash** | a USB-A plug or an old USB cable to cut (5 V from the bike's USB socket) | No | ₹50–100 (est.) |
| Foam tape, hook-up wire, heat shrink | | No | ₹100–200 (est.) |

**Hardware:**
- 2 × M2.5 heat-set inserts (3.6 mm hole) + 2 × M2.5 × 8 screws (the back lid);
- 2 × M3 heat-set inserts (4.2 mm hole, 5.7 mm long) + 2 × M3 × 10 screws (the arm to the lid);
- 2 × M4 heat-set inserts (5.6 mm hole, 8 mm long) + 2 × M4 × 20 bolts (the clamp);
- a 1 mm rubber strip for inside the clamp;
- clear silicone or contact adhesive (gluing the keypad's edge to the face).

About ₹100–250 (est.).

**Printing:** about 30 g in ASA or PETG, plus about 3 g of TPU (the keypad and gasket): about ₹250–500 (est.) from a print service.

**Rough total:** about **₹900–2,000 (est.)** with printing, less with the dash's spare transceiver.
