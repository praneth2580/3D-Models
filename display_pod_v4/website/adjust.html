Adjust it to your hardware

All dimensions live in scad/parameters.scad, each tagged with its source. Values tagged PROVISIONAL are estimates: check them against your own parts before the final print. documentation/measurements.md lists every value and its status.

  1. Install OpenSCAD (2021.01 or newer). It is free.
  2. Edit scad/parameters.scad, then open scad/main.scad to preview the assembly (F5).
  3. Run the checks, then export every part: 
    tools/check.sh     # every clearance and bayonet check must PASS
tools/export.sh    # writes exports/*.stl and *.dxf
Your display boardSets the housing
board_d, board_total_h, board_tab_wBoard outline, height including the USB tab, and tab width. The defaults come from the ESP32-2424S012 drawing: 37.0, 38.5 and 14.23 mm. Change them for a different round board.
display_active_dThe visible screen area (32.4 mm). The window opening follows it.
pcb_t, display_glass_d, rear_component_hBoard thickness, glass diameter and the tallest part on the back, including the battery plug. Measure with calipers. PROVISIONAL
usb_offset_x, usb_plug_w, usb_plug_hUSB-C position, and the size of your cable's moulded plug, so it fits the recess. PROVISIONAL
switch_angle, rst_angle, boot_angleWhere the power switch and buttons sit on your board (angles seen from the front).
BatterySets the rear cover
battery_t, battery_w, battery_lYour LiPo's size (default 402030: 4 × 20 × 30 mm, about 200–250 mAh, including its protection board). Use a cell with protection, and check the plug's polarity. PROVISIONAL
battery_swell_clearRoom left for the cell to swell (1 mm). Don't reduce it.
Your bikeSets the clamp and cup
bar_dHandlebar diameter where the clamp goes (22.2 mm default; most bikes are 22.2, some 25.4 or 28.6 mm). PROVISIONAL
clamp_wClamp width along the bar: fit it to the free straight length between the riser clamps and cables. PROVISIONAL
display_tiltAngle of the display toward you (40° default): set it from your riding position. PROVISIONAL
cup_offset_y, cup_floor_zWhere the pod sits relative to the bar: behind or ahead of it, and how high. Check clearance to the instrument cluster, cables and full steering lock. PROVISIONAL
Hardware and fitMatch what you buy and your printer
detent_ball_dBall size of your M4 spring-ball plunger (2.5 mm). Its stroke must be at least 0.8 mm. PROVISIONAL
insert_hole_dHole for the M2 heat-set inserts (3.2 mm): check your insert brand.
receiver_pilot_dPilot hole for the M3 thread-forming screws.
entry_clear, track_clearBayonet clearances. If the printed fit test is too tight, raise them by 0.05–0.1 mm.
lens_t, gasket_tLens thickness (1 mm polycarbonate) and gasket thickness (1 mm silicone, EPDM or TPU).
